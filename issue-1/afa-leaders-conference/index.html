ABM in action

15 ABM students are headed to Kansas City

AFA Leaders Conference, November 12–15, 2026.

The AFA Leaders Conference is a free leadership and career conference for students headed into food and agriculture. NC State is sending 61 students this year, its largest group ever. Fifteen are ABM, and the department is covering their travel.

The ABM group

Braden Armstrong, Grace Bailey, Elizabeth Bennett, Daniel Davalos-Martinez, Cynthia DeVine, Nithin Gudderra, Andrew Henderson, Nia Long, Paige Meadows, Shannon Purnama, Jacob Rase, Brian Rodriguez, Dylan Rueda Ibanez, Preston Sharp, and Brett Wimberly.

Nithin Gudderra is on AFA's Student Advisory Team and recruited most of the group.

In their words
"I was born in Kansas City, and AFA and NC State have come together to let me go back, not as a tourist, but as an upcoming business professional."
Grace Bailey, ABM '27, ABM Ambassador
"I don't want to measure what I gain from this conference by the number of connections I make, but by how much I grow as a person and how I carry those experiences forward."
Daniel Davalos-Martinez, ABM
