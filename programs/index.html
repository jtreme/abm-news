Start here: scholarships
PACK ASSIST General Scholarship Application
One application that matches you to hundreds of NC State, CALS, and department scholarships. You have to reapply every year. CALS students also answer the CALS questions inside PACK ASSIST, and you should file the FAFSA too, since many awards consider need.
Who: Every undergraduate.
Opens Dec. 1. Priority deadline Feb. 15, 2027.
Go to PACK ASSIST ›

Scholarships for ABM students that PACK ASSIST matches you to include the ADAMA Scholarship (preference for ABM juniors and seniors), the NC Society of Farm Managers and Rural Appraisers Scholarship (first preference to ABM), the Randall V. and Erika W. Canady Scholarship (ARE students headed for agribusiness), and the Albert Gee Willcox Jr. Memorial Agricultural Scholarship. You don’t apply to these separately.

First-years only
Caldwell Fellows
NC State’s four-year servant-leadership program, with training and a cohort of peers. All majors.
Who: Full-time students in their first year at NC State with a 3.25 GPA or higher. You can apply only once.
Due Jan. 11, 2027, 5 p.m. Interviews Feb. 6; Selection Day Feb. 20.

NC State Study Abroad Scholarship Application
One application for more than 20 study abroad awards, including the CALS award ($500–$2,500) and the Chancellor’s Study Abroad Scholarship ($2,500–$4,000). Separate from PACK ASSIST.
Who: Students earning credit abroad who won’t graduate before the program.
Summer 2027 programs: Nov. 15, 2026. Fall 2027 and full year: Feb. 15, 2027.

Research
Project Supply research grants
Funding for a research project with a faculty mentor: up to $500 for supplies and up to $1,000 for your time.
Who: Any undergraduate with a faculty research mentor. You attend a proposal help session first.
Next window Feb. 8 – Mar. 8, 2027 (for summer 2027 projects).
Learn more ›
Research travel awards
Up to $200 toward travel to present your research at a conference, matched by your mentor or department.
Who: Undergraduates accepted to present original research.
Rolling. Apply at least three weeks before you travel.

Watch for these. Dates not posted yet.
  • McNair Scholars Program: prepares first-generation, income-eligible students with a 3.0 GPA or higher for a PhD, with summer research and graduate school support. Last year’s deadline was in March.
  • Paid summer research programs at NC State: the summer 2027 list posts in late fall. Deadlines usually run December through March.
  • Spring Undergraduate Research Symposium, Apr. 27–28, 2027: a good first place to present. The abstract deadline is usually late March.
  • National fellowships such as Truman, Udall, and Fulbright: NC State nominates students, with campus deadlines months ahead of national ones. Talk to the University Fellowships Office early.

Deadlines come from each program’s official NC State page. Check the program’s page before you apply, since dates can change.
