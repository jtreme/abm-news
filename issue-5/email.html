ABM in action
She studied grain markets in an ABM workshop. Now she buys grain for a living.
Paige Smith · ABM, May 2026 · Smithfield Grain
Paige took the six-session workshop taught by a Smithfield grain buyer in her last semester. She accepted a job with Smithfield Grain that April. The workshop earns a certificate and one credit hour, and runs again next fall.
“A farmer will not sell to you if they do not trust you.”
