ABM in action

“Terra-fic” interviews

Eighteen ABM students sat for mock interviews with Terra Green’s president and team.
By Matt Smith, ARE Student Engagement and Support Specialist

Have you ever had the chance to interview with the president of a company? At 10:30 a.m. on Thursday, Oct. 29, 18 Agricultural Business Management students courageously sat for mock interviews with three Terra Green representatives: PJ Faust, the company’s president; Brandon “Brew” Brewington, manager of organizational development and an NC State alum; and ABM alumnus John Andrews. With only a week’s notice, our students came prepared and eager to sharpen how they present themselves. Without realizing it, they also showcased the strength of our department to a leader in the green industry.

One by one, students checked in, picked up their résumés, and entered a conference room for a 20-minute mock interview. They answered employer questions such as, “How have your work and leadership experiences, along with your time at NC State, shaped your interests and goals?” It was clear how much the students enjoyed the experience. They left their interviews feeling empowered.

“The interview went very well, and Brandon was a very nice guy. He told me things I did well and things I could work on. He even pointed out a small error on my résumé, which was very helpful.”
Ryan Martinez, ABM junior

People often feel nervous before an interview. Talking about ourselves can be difficult because we worry too much about what others might think of us, which creates self-imposed pressure. We get inside our own heads with conflicting thoughts about what to say and how to say it, instead of learning how to confidently articulate who we are.

“What I will take with me in the future is to not shy away from what I do and to be ready to tell my story.”
Yarethsi Rangel, ABM junior

Students said they relaxed once the interview started, and they described how helpful the practice was. Several encouraged their peers to take part.

“I would tell students that it’s always beneficial to do a practice interview. They tell you what you can work on and what you did well so you’re ready for a real interview,” Ryan said.

“I would definitely recommend this to anybody and everybody who can attend. This serves as an opportunity to not only get real experience with the all too important interview but also acts as practice with no stakes involved.”
Franklin Holt, ABM freshman
From the other side of the table

An ABM alumnus comes back

For John Andrews, the morning was a return to Nelson Hall.

“Returning to campus as an alumnus to participate in the mock interviews was a really rewarding experience. I was incredibly impressed by the students, not only by their professionalism, but also by their involvement and accomplishments both inside and outside the classroom. It was exciting to see the next generation of ABM graduates and future professionals, and it was a great reminder of how much I enjoyed my time at NC State. I always love having the opportunity to come back and give back to the university.”
John Andrews, Terra Green, ABM alumnus
From Terra Green

“An honor to invest in the next generation”

Terra Green, a landscape company with deep NC State roots, also offers a summer internship built around hands-on experience and leadership development.

“We were excited to return to campus last week and partner with the university to support student development. Since Terra Green has deep NC State roots, including senior leaders who are proud alumni, it was an honor to invest in the next generation of green industry leaders through the 18 mock interviews we conducted. We believe that helping students build confidence and career skills directly impacts their future. Similarly, our summer internship is designed to give students real-life, hands-on experience and extensive leadership development. It was a pleasure working with Dr. Treme, Matt, and the team throughout this event, and we look forward to partnering with them again soon. Go Pack!”
Brandon “Brew” Brewington, Terra Green
