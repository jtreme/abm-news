Peyton Sharp interned at the North Carolina Supreme Court
Peyton in the courtroom of the Supreme Court of North Carolina.
Last spring, Peyton worked in Justice Trey Allen's chambers at the state's highest court. She drafted petitions for discretionary review, researched cases, wrote memos for the chambers, and sat in on oral arguments. She found it through a LinkedIn post, then wrote to every justice directly to ask about openings. Peyton is a senior from Clayton with a minor in Agricultural Entrepreneurship and is ABM Club social chair. Next up: law school.
“You do not have to know everything or have the perfect resume to be deserving of an opportunity.”
Peyton Sharp, ABM ’27

Good to know
Take ARE 301 first, then ARE 336
Building your spring schedule? Don't take ARE 336 before ARE 301, and don't take them in the same semester. Finish 301 first. If 336 is on your spring schedule and you haven't completed 301, swap it out now and talk with your advisor.
