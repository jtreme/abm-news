ABM in action

The Crop Dusters made a playoff run

ABM’s intramural basketball team came within one win of the Elite Eight.
‹ Back to Issue 3
From left: Jake Everett, Austin Collins, Owen Tucker, Kore Wallace, Ben Neal, Athan Pilos, Brayden Frederick, and coach Brooke Myers.

ABM’s intramural basketball team went 2–2 in the regular season, won their first playoff game, and lost in the second round by just four points, one win from the Elite Eight.

Owen Tucker (ABM) and Kore Wallace led the team in scoring every game. Brooke Myers coached the team, scheduled the games, and kept track of the jerseys.

“Just join the team; there’s nothing to lose! Everyone has different skill levels, and that is perfectly fine.”
Brooke Myers, Crop Dusters coach
