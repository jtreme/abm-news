Roots Up Wellness: Elena Wigglesworth pitched from a balcony and won

By Matt Smith, Student Engagement and Support Specialist, Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics

Afraid of heights? Elena Wigglesworth isn't. She's here to combine her interests and push her innovation to the fullest, even if it means pitching her ideas 12 feet above the ground.

On September 17, Elena competed in Minute to Pitch It, a competition held at Entrepalooza, NC State's annual entrepreneurship and innovation festival. It's hosted by NC State Innovation and Entrepreneurship, which supports students across the university in developing ideas, solving problems, and starting ventures. Its mission emphasizes practical experience and collaboration across different fields.

Elena was influenced by a connection she had in her scholarship program. She signed up to push herself to keep building her business. "It forced me to talk about my business. A 60-second pitch, I had to articulate it." What she didn't expect was to deliver her pitch from a balcony 12 feet above the judges and audience in Stafford Commons. Called to present second, Elena put herself on the line. She received a $500 award for Roots Up Wellness.

The business

Roots Up Wellness aims to bring yoga, fitness classes, meditation, and retreats to local farms and green spaces, including parks and recreation spaces. It combines her passions for agriculture and wellness.

Elena's interests in fitness and agriculture have been developing for years. A competitive swimmer growing up, she started teaching swimming at 11 and continued for a decade. After deferring admission, she worked on farms around the world, including a vegetable farm in Ireland and a permaculture-based wellness center in Switzerland. She came back wanting to tell the story of agriculture. Roots Up Wellness gives her a way to share that story while helping people take care of themselves. Her long-term goal is to open an agriculture-based community wellness center.

What's next

On October 22, Elena pitched Roots Up Wellness to Ronnen Harary, co-founder of Spin Master, through his No Experience Necessary Challenge. She is now being considered for a higher-stakes competition in December, with $25,000 at stake.

Elena plans to graduate in May 2028 with a double major in agricultural business and parks and recreation, with a concentration in tourism and event planning. That combination has prompted questions like "Are you gonna plan animal weddings?"

"Start now!"
Elena Wigglesworth, ABM '28, on her advice for students thinking about starting a business. She sees NC State as a safe place to begin, with a bubble of resources to help students develop their ideas.
