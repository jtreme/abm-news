Good to know

Answers you may need later

A class you need is full? We can’t add you to classes outside ARE, including PE. Get on the waitlist and go the first day, take it at a community college, or take it another semester. Take ARE 301 first, then ARE 336 Students who finish 301 first come into 336 better prepared, with the concepts and math practice 301 gives them. Internship credit: ARE 492 An internship can count toward Restricted Electives. Send the completed form to Matt Smith. PACK ASSIST opens Dec. 1 One application for hundreds of scholarships. Reapply every year.
