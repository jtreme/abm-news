05 / Good to know

Questions, answered

Are the ARE and university ceremonies the same event?

No. ARE’s ceremony is Friday, December 11, at noon in Nelson Hall, Room 3400. University Commencement is Saturday, December 12, at 9 a.m. in the Lenovo Center. Follow the arrangements for each event you plan to attend.

How many guest tickets do I get, and where do I pick them up?

Six physical tickets per graduate. Pick them up in person from Matt Smith in Nelson Hall, Office 3341, starting Monday, December 1. Every guest needs a ticket to enter Room 3400.

How do I RSVP for the ARE ceremony?

Complete the graduation survey by Thursday, December 10 at 5 p.m. You must tell us you are coming so we can have your slide ready when you walk. University Commencement has a separate RSVP and ticket process.

Go to Tickets & RSVP ↑
What time should graduates and guests arrive?

Graduates meet in Room 4305 at 11:15 a.m. Guests should be seated in Room 3400 before the ceremony begins at noon.

Will there be a livestream?

Yes. The ceremony will be livestreamed. The link will be sent to graduates before December 11 so they can share it with family and friends who cannot attend.

Where is the accessible entrance to Nelson Hall, Room 3400?

An accessible entrance with a powered door opener is in the west courtyard on the Dan Allen Drive side of Nelson Hall, near the accessible parking spaces. Once inside, take the elevator to the third floor to reach Room 3400.

For other accommodations at the ARE ceremony, contact Matt Smith or Julianne Treme. The university’s accessibility guide applies to the separate Lenovo Center ceremony.

Get in touch ↓
Does attending the ceremony mean my degree is complete?

Graduation is the completion of degree requirements; commencement is the celebration. Review your degree audit and application status with your advisor. Diplomas are issued after final clearance, rather than at the ceremony.
